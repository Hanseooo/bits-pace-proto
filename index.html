DESIGN CRITERIA
PACEProgress-led identity
BITSBuilder-led identity
Core idea
Growth through learning and achievement. Every step builds toward technical excellence.
Creativity through making. Small ideas turn into useful tools and shared projects.
Personality
Focused, structured, aspirational, academic.
Curious, playful, practical, experimental.
Name clarity
Names programmers and computing excellence.
Names technology and software builders.
Strongest use cases
Workshops, competitions, mentorship, and academic partnerships.
Hackathons, team projects, open source, and creative coding.
Cross-course reach
Open to students interested in programming and computing.
Welcomes makers from many courses. Software remains the focus.
Potential weakness
May feel formal to beginners without welcoming community messaging.
May feel informal unless communications show its academic and technical scope.
Brand system
Ascending paths, structured grids, cool hues, restrained editorial typography.
Pixel-built symbols, bold contrasts, modular blocks, expressive typographic layouts.
